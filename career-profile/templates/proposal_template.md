# Tailored Job Proposal Template

Use this structure when generating a proposal from a job description:

Paragraph 1:
- State the exact role title.
- Mention the strongest matching experience in one or two sentences.
- Keep it direct and specific.

Paragraph 2:
- Reference the most relevant stack, domain, or project experience.
- Mention one or two concrete outcomes when available.
- Do not list everything from the profile.

Paragraph 3:
- Close briefly with the next step. State availability or relocation details only when confirmed and relevant.
- Keep the ending concise.

Rules:
- Keep the proposal short.
- Tailor every paragraph to the provided job description.
- Avoid generic phrases such as "I am passionate" unless the role explicitly values them.
- Prefer direct evidence over adjectives.
- Do not claim experience that is not present in the profile files.
- If critical details are missing, say that the proposal should stay conservative.
